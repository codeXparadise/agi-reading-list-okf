# Sections

The five thematic groups of the original list, in order.

## Groups

* [Core Architecture & Transformers](01-core-architecture-and-transformers.md) - The Transformer and the convolutional architectures that preceded and surround it: attention, scaling behaviour, ResNets and dilated convolutions.
* [Recurrent Neural Networks](02-recurrent-neural-networks.md) - Sequence modelling before and alongside attention: RNNs, LSTMs, regularisation, pointer networks, attention-based translation and end-to-end speech.
* [Reasoning & External Memory](03-reasoning-and-external-memory.md) - Giving a network somewhere to write and something to reason over: differentiable memory and relational reasoning modules.
* [Theory, Complexity & Optimization](04-theory-complexity-and-optimization.md) - How models are trained and why they generalise: optimisers, regularisation, scaling systems, minimum description length, Kolmogorov complexity and the theory of intelligence.
* [Generative Models & Additional Domains](05-generative-models-and-additional-domains.md) - Generating data rather than labelling it, and applying message passing to scientific domains.
