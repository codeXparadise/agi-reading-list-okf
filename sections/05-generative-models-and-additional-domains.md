# Generative Models & Additional Domains

Generating data rather than labelling it, and applying message passing to scientific domains.

## Papers

1. [28 - Generative Adversarial Nets](/papers/28-generative-adversarial-nets.md) - Ian J. Goodfellow, Jean Pouget-Abadie et al. (2014). Two networks trained against each other: a generator that produces samples and a discriminator that tries to tell them from real data.
2. [29 - Variational Lossy Autoencoder](/papers/29-variational-lossy-autoencoder.md) - Xi Chen, Diederik P. Kingma et al. (2016). Combines variational autoencoders with expressive autoregressive decoders and explicit control over what the latent code is allowed to keep, giving much sharper generative models and a clean information-theoretic reading of why..
3. [30 - Neural Message Passing for Quantum Chemistry](/papers/30-neural-message-passing-for-quantum-chemistry.md) - Justin Gilmer, Samuel S. Schoenholz et al. (2017). Shows that a wide family of graph neural networks is really one message-passing algorithm, and sets a strong benchmark on molecular property prediction..
