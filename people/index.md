# People

The researchers behind the papers, with the papers each one wrote in this bundle.

## Researchers

* [Ashish Vaswani](ashish-vaswani.md) - Lead author of 'Attention Is All You Need'.
* [Ilya Sutskever](ilya-sutskever.md) - Co-author of AlexNet, sequence-to-sequence learning and Deep Speech 2.
* [Geoffrey E. Hinton](geoffrey-hinton.md) - Co-author of AlexNet, dropout and the 1993 minimum-description-length paper.
* [Kaiming He](kaiming-he.md) - Author of ResNet and its pre-activation follow-up, the papers that made very deep networks trainable..
* [Oriol Vinyals](oriol-vinyals.md) - Co-author of sequence-to-sequence learning, pointer networks, order matters, relational RNNs and neural message passing for chemistry..
* [Andrej Karpathy](andrej-karpathy.md) - Author of 'The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Recurrent Neural Networks', the essay that introduced a generation to character-level language modelling..
* [Christopher Olah](christopher-olah.md) - Author of 'Understanding LSTM Networks', the standard visual explanation of gated recurrence..
* [Alexander M. Rush](alexander-rush.md) - Author of the Annotated Transformer and maintainer of Harvard NLP's teaching material on sequence models..
* [Dzmitry Bahdanau](dzmitry-bahdanau.md) - Lead author of the paper that introduced attention to neural machine translation..
* [Ian J. Goodfellow](ian-goodfellow.md) - Lead author of 'Generative Adversarial Nets'..
* [Diederik P. Kingma](diederik-kingma.md) - Lead author of Adam and co-author of the variational lossy autoencoder..
* [Scott Aaronson](scott-aaronson.md) - Theoretical computer scientist.
* [Peter D. Grunwald](peter-grunwald.md) - Author of the MDL tutorial and of the standard reference book on minimum description length..
* [Shane Legg](shane-legg.md) - Author of the 'Machine Super Intelligence' thesis and co-founder of DeepMind..
* [Alex Graves](alex-graves.md) - Lead author of Neural Turing Machines.
* [Adam Santoro](adam-santoro.md) - Lead author of the relation-network and relational-RNN papers..
* [Dario Amodei](dario-amodei.md) - Co-author of Deep Speech 2 and of the scaling-laws paper.
* [Alex Krizhevsky](alex-krizhevsky.md) - Lead author of AlexNet and co-author of the dropout paper..
* [Yanping Huang](yanping-huang.md) - Lead author of GPipe..
* [Justin Gilmer](justin-gilmer.md) - Lead author of 'Neural Message Passing for Quantum Chemistry'..
* [Xi Chen](xi-chen.md) - Lead author of the variational lossy autoencoder..
* [Wojciech Zaremba](wojciech-zaremba.md) - Lead author of 'Recurrent Neural Network Regularization'..
* [Alexander Shen](alexander-shen.md) - Co-author of 'Kolmogorov Complexity and Algorithmic Randomness'..
