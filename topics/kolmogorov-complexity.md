# Kolmogorov Complexity

The length of the shortest program that produces a given object.

## Why it matters in this list

Kolmogorov complexity is the uncomputable ideal behind every compression-based argument about learning and complexity in this list.

## Papers

- [22 - The First Law of Complexodynamics](/papers/22-first-law-of-complexodynamics.md) (2011) - An essay asking for a law that explains why the 'complexity' of a closed system rises, peaks and falls while entropy only rises - and proposing resource-bounded Kolmogorov complexity as the measure that would make such a law precise..
- [24 - A Tutorial Introduction to the Minimum Description Length Principle](/papers/24-tutorial-minimum-description-length.md) (2004) - A readable tutorial on MDL: learning as finding the model that compresses the data shortest, with the theory of why that controls overfitting.
- [25 - Quantifying the Rise and Fall of Complexity in Closed Systems: The Coffee Automaton](/papers/25-coffee-automaton.md) (2014) - Turns the coffee-and-milk thought experiment into a simulation, separating entropy from 'apparent complexity' and measuring how the latter rises and then falls as the two liquids mix..
- [26 - Kolmogorov Complexity and Algorithmic Randomness](/papers/26-kolmogorov-complexity-and-algorithmic-randomness.md) (2017) - The standard graduate text on Kolmogorov complexity, randomness deficiency and the two-part-code view of a good model.
- [27 - Machine Super Intelligence](/papers/27-machine-super-intelligence.md) (2008) - Defines universal intelligence formally as expected performance across all computable environments, weighted by simplicity, and follows the consequences for agents, compression and safety.
