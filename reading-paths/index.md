# Reading paths

Three curated routes through the list. Use one if you want an order rather than a menu.

## Routes

* [Orientation in Seven Days](orientation-seven-days.md) - A one-week pass over the list for someone who wants the shape of modern AI quickly: what the Transformer is, what came before it, and what the theory underneath says.
* [Sequence Models to Transformers](sequence-models-to-transformers.md) - A focused track through the sequence-modelling lineage: RNNs and LSTMs, seq2seq, attention, and the Transformer that removed recurrence entirely.
* [Theory and Compression](theory-and-compression.md) - The non-engineering half of the list: description length, Kolmogorov complexity, the complexity of closed systems, and a formal definition of intelligence.
