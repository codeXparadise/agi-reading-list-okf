# Theory and Compression

The non-engineering half of the list: description length, Kolmogorov complexity, the complexity of closed systems, and a formal definition of intelligence.

This track is why the list is not just a list of architectures. It argues that learning is compression, and gives the mathematics for that claim.

## Description length

- [23 - Keeping Neural Networks Simple by Minimizing the Description Length of the Weights](/papers/23-keeping-neural-networks-simple.md) (1993, COLT 1993)
- [24 - A Tutorial Introduction to the Minimum Description Length Principle](/papers/24-tutorial-minimum-description-length.md) (2004, arXiv math/0406077)

Weights as distributions, then the MDL principle in full.

## Complexity

- [26 - Kolmogorov Complexity and Algorithmic Randomness](/papers/26-kolmogorov-complexity-and-algorithmic-randomness.md) (2017, AMS Mathematical Surveys and Monographs 220)
- [22 - The First Law of Complexodynamics](/papers/22-first-law-of-complexodynamics.md) (2011, Shtetl-Optimized (blog essay))
- [25 - Quantifying the Rise and Fall of Complexity in Closed Systems: The Coffee Automaton](/papers/25-coffee-automaton.md) (2014, arXiv)

Kolmogorov complexity, the proposed first law of complexodynamics, and the simulation that tries to measure it.

Universal intelligence as performance across all computable environments, weighted by simplicity.
