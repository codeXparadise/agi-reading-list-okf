# The 30 Papers to AI Mastery

Source: the PDF *The AGI Reading List* by @rachit.speaks, which presents the 30 papers as "the list Ilya Sutskever gave John Carmack" with the claim that learning all of them covers 90% of what matters in AI today. The list is reproduced here in its published order and grouping; each entry links to its concept document.

## Note on provenance

The published list is a reconstruction. Neither Sutskever nor OpenAI has published an authoritative version, and public copies describe it as partial: John Carmack has said the original email was lost and that the list ran to roughly forty papers. Treat the numbering in this bundle as the numbering of the published PDF, not as an official ranking.
